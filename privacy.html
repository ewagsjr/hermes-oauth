Hermes Privacy Policy

Hermes is a private, single-user app. It accesses Google user data only for the account owner who authorizes it, only on the owner's own computer, and only to perform tasks the owner requests.

Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
